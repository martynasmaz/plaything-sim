# Local progression update — 14 November 2026

This version includes the accumulated homestead work: needs-based priorities,
fishing equipment and visuals, persistent NPC-built healer homes and succession,
optional typed-only folk magic, bone fertilizer, birth observation, woodland and
building clearings, compact standing places, corner information cards, and
phone-specific controls.

Food readiness now counts accessible finished stores. Meals use local preserved
food during a main-pantry shortfall, preventing stocked households from taking
their neighbors' only meal. Villagers plan physical housing before every bed is
occupied. Paid visits link existing communities through teaching and unrelated
families; no outside rescue population is created.

The new cereal progression supplements existing gardens with wheat, physical
saddle querns, wholemeal flour and fuel-paid hearth flatbread. It aims for useful
food processing around day 500 through prerequisites and ordinary work rather
than an automatic discovery. Knowledge can pass through completed paired lessons,
and children can observe the actual new work. Existing saves retain their fields,
discoveries, people and projects.

Validation: all 278 tests passed, including original reference replays, current
food and family rules, cereal prerequisites, real ingredient consumption, finite
storage, persistent querns, urgent interruptions, paired teaching and save/load.
The offline build compiled and parsed. Native Canvas checks rendered wheat and
querns without changing simulation state and exercised phone/desktop pointer
selection. These do not claim an actual browser or phone-device test.

Ten fresh hands-off seeds were checked to day 1,200. The measured progression is
in `reports/wheat-progression-10-20261114.json` and its Markdown companion. Earlier
population reports remain historical measurements of their source versions,
before cereals. Their day-3,000 percentages are not current wheat-version odds.
Long-term food balance and reliable transmission of specialist knowledge still
need further work; wheat is not a guaranteed survival switch.
